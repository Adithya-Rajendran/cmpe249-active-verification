# Workshop scene assets

The room uses four photographed color maps from Poly Haven. The study resizes and tints these maps. Poly Haven releases its assets under CC0. [Asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

| Surface | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Floor and worn marking | [Worn concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001) |
| Painted masonry | [Painted brick](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_brick) |
| Sliding door | [Painted metal shutter](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_metal_shutter) |
| Shelf decks | [Plywood](https://polyhaven.com/a/plywood) |

The workshop supplies its own signs, carton labels, racks, cabinet and door hardware.
The two renderers use the same color maps and different lighting models.
The scene uses photographed YCB meshes for the drill, bottle and clamp. [Object authors, licenses and distribution](../../paper/evidence/ASSET-CREDITS.md)

The material inventory records the source URLs and SHA-256 checksums. The generator records each emitted texture checksum. [Material inventory](materials/sources.json) · [Material generator](materials.py)
